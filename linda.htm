WANTED
HELP FIND THE FOLLOWING LOCATIONS

THE AVENGERS: Requiem

Although this episode features a number of known locations, there are still a couple be traced.

Here is the building in which Tara overheard the plot to kill Mother. The detail on the left shows that this is a dark brick building. Tara runs past the ornate doorway and collapses in the Yellow Rolls Royce. We see a low wall with a greenhouse beyond suggesting this might be a side door.

The final missing location in this episode is the building that poses as the hospital where Tara is held. All we see of this is an angular wing and the doorway reflected in the Roll's lamp. We see the grounds as the Rolls drives away but this is going to be hard work to find either of these buildings (or could they both be the same building).

Good luck.

Posted 17th April 2000

THE AVENGERS: Who Was That Man I Saw You With?

The episode starts with a sequence with Tara King breaking into the War Room. We see a long honey-coloured wall with a gateway set in to it. Again this might be close to Primrose Hill (Wyn Roberts suggests it might be by Hampstead Ponds). Major merit points on offer to anyone spotting these locations.

 

Two scenes also use the same location and camera position: Tara is followed to her rendezvous at the telephone box and later Fairfax follows Tara to the car park. Primrose Hill or Hampstead Heath?

THE AVENGERS: You have Just Been Murdered and Homicide and Old Lace

The offices of Unwin Enterprises and Intercrime are the same. This is a large complex with a large underground car park. One can make out the name "..ENGROVE HOUSE" above the door. This is a major location which could be anywhere between Borehamwood and Central London. My bet is that is a Polytechnic or University building.

THE AVENGERS: Game and The See-Through Man

The same childrens play area appears in both these episodes. Set in acres of mature parkland this is probably situated in a large town close to the studios. Anyone growing up in the area must remember such a comprehensive selection of serious play equipment.

THE AVENGERS: The Interrogators

Tara pays an unsuccessful visit to Minnow's home but is too late to stop him from being hurried away by Colonel Mannering.

This is obviously London - but where?

Steed follows Caspar back to his home.

The blue sign to the left of the gatepost reads "subway", This and the architecture reminds me of the area around Watford Town Hall. I could, as usual, be totally wrong.
