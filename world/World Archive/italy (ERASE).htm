THE PROTECTORS:See No Evil (Jeremy Summers: Summer 1971)
From her Rolls, Caroline (Nyree Dawn Porter) (in the Piazza Navona) is tracking a suspect as he approaches the Via del Sanata by the radio transmitter in his shoe. Harry Rule (Robert Vaughn) is monitoring Caroline’s progress from across the piazza in his car. The suspect is lost when he dumps his shoes in a garbage bin. Chino drives Caroline and Harry, in the Rolls, to see Senator Gordini in a café on the Via Veneto.

Later an assassin (Via del Portico d'Ottavia, in the Ghetto) attempts to kill The Contessa (Nyree Dawn Porter), but is unsuccessful. He runs away pursued by Chino (Anthony Chinn).
The assassin is frog marched by The Contessa (Nyree Dawn Porter) and Chino (Anthony Chinn) past some Roman ruins (Theatre of Marcellus) to be interrogated. The Assassin takes The Contessa (Nyree Dawn Porter) to a meeting with a blind man (Alan Webb) (VicoloCostaguti and the Carmel Temple, the temple has been completely cleaned and restored in 2004. You can see what these locations look like now at Andrea Pollett’s page).


via de' Delfini, Photo: Andrea Pollett

Harry (Robert Vaughn) meets up with a contact outside a church (via de' Delfini) and is bundled off in a van. Harry is driven in the back of a van to see Lucello, the Mr. Big of the Roman underworld (This is PiazzaleUgo La Malfa, along the western side of the old Circus Maximus area. The actual arena is below the ground level, as the road runs where the tiers once stood, the remains of the imperial palace are clearly in sight. The white artefact, partly seen, is the base of the monument of Giuseppe Mazzini). Harry continues his journey driven in the back of a van (Via Veneto looking towards its northern end, the Pinciana Gate (Aurelian wall), seen at the bottom of the street.)


Via di San Michele, Photo: Andrea Pollett

Harry eventually reaches his final destination. Soon after The Contessa (Nyree Dawn Porter) is led to the same location by the blind man (Alan Webb) (The street is called via di San Michele, after the large complex on the left (San Michele), once a hospice and a house of correction for teenagers since the early 19th century, then (1980s) turned into the headquarters of the Central Institute for Restoration).

THE PERSUADERS!:Five Miles to Midnight
Opening view establishing "ROME" is from the Piazza del Popolo looking towards the Vatican.

 

Brett (Roger Moore) jaunts around Rome driving his Aston Martin to Sidonie's well soundproofed flat. On the way he passes the Vittorio Emanuele II monument and drives up Piazza di Venezia, he passes the Colosseum, the Quirinale Palace (south end of Via del Quirinale), along the Via Vittorio Venetta (arches at end of road).

    

The main part of the episode in Rome was filmed around the surrounding streets of the Vatican. Unfortunately, I could not find the exact streets but this was confirmed by Carl Seagar that it was around this area that the crew filmed here. Carl was the driver supplied by Aston Martin for filming on the series.
Note: This building* is also seen in the opening titles of THE PROTECTORS when Nyree Dawn Porter is introduced.
(Information courtesy of Jaz Wiseman, some more detail added by Paul Stroud)

THE PERSUADERS!: The Man in the Middle
Brett is again seen driving his Aston Martin around the Coliseum. Obviously this piece of film was from the same sequence used in Five Miles to Midnight. He also drives past the Triumphal Arch of Constantine and up the Via de S. Gregorio. The Arch was built in A.D. 315 and celebrates the Emperor Constantine's victory over his rival Maxentius. (A bit of history never hurt anybody!). The final time we see Brett driving in Rome he is travelling down the Via D. Concilliazione with St Peters clearly visible in the background. This is the main approach road to St Peters and is obviously very busy, so if you do go there watch out for the Italian drivers!
(Information courtesy of Jaz Wiseman)
