Sidonie and Frank Rocco are dropped off and told to make their way to the rendezvous on foot. Brett and Danny continue to drive the old van, but when it blows a tyre they retreat to a derelict chapel on the hillside (Chapel de Saint Pancrace). Torino's men arrive and a shoot out begins. Sidonie and Frank find themselves a mile from the rendezvous and contemplate Brett and Danny's situation. Torino's men suddenly retreat and drive off as a police car arrives.

When Grimer makes his escape in a stolen wagon Gambit chases after him in a rubbish wagon and is given directions by Purdey from the roof. After a long chase, Grimer is captured by Gambit (on the Rue President Wilson steps).
