THE PROTECTORS: It was all Over in Leipzig: (Don Chaffey: Summer 1971)
Harry Rule (Robert Vaughn) is at a road block to prevent arms moving across the city. He spots a girl (Tanya Bayona) with a baby carriage, it is filled with grenades which spill out was it crashes down some stairs. The girl is killed by a sniper (Paul Weston) before she can give Harry any information (sniper shoots from balcony by The Grand Harbour Hotel, Triqu Sant Ursula). Harry (Robert Vaughn) spots the assassin (Paul Weston) and chases him across town through some gardens (Upper Barakka Gardens) before he climbs down the ramparts and escapes in a pickup truck.

Later Jim Palmer (Ron Randall) arrives in the harbour and makes his way towards The Dolphin, The Contessa’s yacht (which in Kalkara Creek facing Vittoriosa).
(Ryan McGuinness, June 2005)
